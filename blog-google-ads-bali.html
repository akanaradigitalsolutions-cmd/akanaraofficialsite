Google Ads for Bali businesses: a practical start

PublishedAug 2026
Read6 min read
ByAkanara

Google Ads can be the fastest way to grow a Bali business — or the fastest way to waste money. The difference is almost never the budget. It's the setup.

Start with intent, not reach

The magic of search ads is that people are already looking for what you offer. Begin with the exact terms a ready-to-buy customer would type — "villa cleaning Canggu", "laundry service Seminyak" — not broad awareness campaigns. Intent first, reach later.

Track conversions before you spend

Never run ads you can't measure. Set up GA4 and Google Tag Manager, and define what a real result is — a booking, a call, a form submission — before the first rupiah goes out. Otherwise you're optimising blind.

A simple first campaign

  • One tightly-themed campaign around your best service
  • A handful of high-intent keywords, exact and phrase match
  • A dedicated landing page that matches the ad's promise
  • Conversion tracking wired to the action that makes you money

Measure, then scale

Give it a couple of weeks of clean data, then double down on what converts and cut what doesn't. Growth comes from disciplined iteration, not from a bigger budget on day one.

Spend small, measure everything, then scale what works.
