Relaxha

A booking marketplace and operator extranet for Bali's wellness scene.

Year2024
RoleProduct · Design · Build
ServicesPlatform · Booking Engine · Web App
OVERVIEW

A direct channel for Bali wellness

Bali's spas and studios were stuck between phone-and-WhatsApp bookings and high OTA commissions. Relaxha gives them a direct channel — and gives guests one place to discover and book.

APPROACH

Two sides, one system

We designed a fast, mobile-first booking flow for guests and a self-serve extranet for operators to manage listings, availability, and orders — architected to scale across many venues.

OUTCOME

Live, and built to grow

A live two-sided platform with clean measurement in place from day one — so bookings and retention can be grown deliberately, not guessed at.
