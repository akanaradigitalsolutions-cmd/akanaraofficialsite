Why website speed is your best marketing channel

PublishedAug 2026
Read5 min read
ByAkanara

Speed isn't a technical detail you hand to a developer and forget. It's one of the highest-leverage marketing decisions you can make — and most businesses leave it on the table.

Speed is conversion

Every second of load time costs you customers. People abandon slow pages before they ever see your offer. A site that loads in under two seconds simply keeps more of the visitors you already paid to attract.

Speed is SEO

Google measures real-world loading experience (Core Web Vitals) and factors it into rankings. A fast, stable page is easier to crawl, easier to rank, and easier to keep ranked.

Speed is cheaper ads

Ad platforms reward good landing-page experience with lower costs. A faster page can mean a higher quality score on Google Ads — which means you pay less for the same clicks. Speed pays for itself twice.

What actually makes a site fast

  • Lean, hand-built code instead of heavy page-builder bloat
  • Images sized and served in modern formats (WebP/AVIF)
  • Fonts and scripts loaded deliberately, not all at once
  • Caching and a CDN doing the heavy lifting
The fastest marketing budget is the one you don't have to spend because your site already converts.
